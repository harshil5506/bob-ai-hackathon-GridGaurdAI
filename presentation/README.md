## Slide Structure (8 Corporate-Grade Slides)

1. **Slide 1: Title & Vision** — Project name, team members, track, value proposition, and compliance badges.
2. **Slide 2: The Problem** — Who is affected, what is failing, and why it hurts ($1M–$2.5M/hr, 4–18 mo lead time, 70% reactive budget drain, 500+ alarms/hr).
3. **Slide 3: The Solution** — What we built: 4-stage physics-grounded mechanism (Data Fusion, IEEE/IEC Scoring, Severity Ranking, Proactive Crew Staging).
4. **Slide 4: Technical Architecture** — 4-tier microservices topology, data flow, and key technical highlights.
5. **Slide 5: Live Demo** — Screenshots and operational walkthrough of the Common Grid Dashboard, Asset Telemetry, and Clearance Studio.
6. **Slide 6: IBM Technology Integration** — Deep dive into IBM Bob CLI, Model Context Protocol (MCP), watsonx.ai foundation models, and Cloud Code Engine.
7. **Slide 7: Business Impact & Future Roadmap** — 42% SAIDI reduction, $85M/yr regional savings, and 3-phase production roadmap.
8. **Slide 8: Team & Submission Summary** — Team member contributions, repository links, and final summary.
